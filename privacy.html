1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Paper Gold handles information. Paper Gold is a cultural interaction and reflection app for iOS.

This policy covers the Paper Gold app and this privacy policy website. Apple, iCloud, the App Store, RevenueCat, and GitHub operate under their own privacy practices.

2. Information Handled by the App

Paper Gold does not require you to create an app account or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising identifier.

The app handles only the information needed for its features: local preferences and unfinished progress, completed ritual records and achievements, and purchase and entitlement information. These categories are described below.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Paper Gold stores preferences and unfinished ritual state locally on your device. This may include:

  • sound and haptic settings;
  • accessibility preferences such as reduce motion or increased contrast;
  • language, selected paper, offering quantity, and selected intention settings;
  • daily and lifetime counters used by the interface; and
  • progress for an offering that has not yet been completed.

This local data is not sent to the developer or RevenueCat. Deleting the app removes its local copy from that device. Depending on your Apple device settings, local app data may also be included in Apple-managed device or iCloud backups.

4. iCloud Sync for Ritual Records and Achievements

When the device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud access is enabled for Paper Gold, the app uses Apple's CloudKit service to store completed ritual records and achievement unlocks in the user's private iCloud database. This allows the information to remain available offline and synchronize across devices using the same Apple Account.

A completed ritual record may include a record identifier, date and time, burn and stack counts, paper type, offering destination, selected intention, optional custom intention text entered by the user, and values used to display the ritual ledger. Achievement records include the achievement type and unlock date.

CloudKit private database content is accessible to the user through the app and, by default, only through the user's Apple Account. The developer cannot view the contents of a user's private CloudKit database in the CloudKit developer portal. Apple processes and stores this data as the iCloud service provider, and it counts toward the user's iCloud storage quota.

If iCloud is unavailable or disabled for Paper Gold, the app continues to store and use its local copy, but changes will not synchronize across devices until iCloud becomes available again. You can control which apps use iCloud in Apple Account > iCloud settings.

5. Purchases, Apple, and RevenueCat

Paper Gold offers two separate optional one-time in-app purchases: Grandma Wang's Paper Bundle and Benming Money. Apple processes both payments. Paper Gold does not receive payment card or bank account details.

The app uses the RevenueCat SDK to validate Grandma Wang's Paper Bundle, determine whether its entitlement is active, and support restoring that purchase. Benming Money uses Apple's StoreKit 2 directly to load the product, validate its transaction, and restore access. RevenueCat receives purchase history and entitlement information from Apple together with a randomly generated anonymous RevenueCat App User ID. RevenueCat may also process limited transaction context such as product identifiers, store, locale, and currency.

This purchase information is used for app functionality, receipt validation, fraud prevention, customer history, and purchase analytics. Paper Gold does not provide RevenueCat with ritual records, custom intention text, contact information, or an advertising identifier. The purchase information is not used by Paper Gold for cross-app advertising tracking.

For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Device Permissions

Paper Gold does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, health, photo library, Bluetooth, or tracking permissions.

Audio is generated and played locally by the app. The app does not use the microphone.

7. Tracking, Ads, and Analytics

Paper Gold does not display ads, use advertising SDKs, request App Tracking Transparency permission, or track activity across other companies' apps or websites.

Paper Gold does not include a general-purpose third-party analytics SDK. RevenueCat processes purchase history for entitlement functionality and purchase analytics as described above. Ritual activity is not sent to RevenueCat for analytics.

Paper Gold does not sell personal information.

8. If You Contact the Developer

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the contents of your message will be used only to respond to your request, provide support, or handle privacy questions. This correspondence is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

9. Privacy Policy Website Hosting

This privacy policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When a GitHub Pages site is visited, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub's handling of this technical data is described in GitHub's own documentation and Privacy Statement.

Paper Gold does not add cookies, analytics scripts, advertising scripts, or tracking pixels to this privacy policy website.

10. Children's Privacy

Paper Gold is not designed to request personal contact information from children or adults. The app does not use advertising or cross-app tracking. Information stored locally, in the user's private iCloud database, or as part of an optional purchase is handled as described in this policy.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on a device until it is removed through app deletion or other device controls.

Deleting Paper Gold removes its local data from that device but may not automatically remove records already stored in iCloud. Users can disable Paper Gold's iCloud access or view and delete third-party app data through their Apple Account and iCloud storage settings. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their respective policies and as needed to validate or restore purchases.

Email correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, or comply with legal obligations.

12. Your Choices

  • You can use Paper Gold without creating an app account.
  • You can control app preferences inside the app.
  • You can control Paper Gold's iCloud access in Apple Account > iCloud settings.
  • You can delete the app to remove its local data from a device.
  • You can manage third-party app data through Apple's iCloud storage controls.
  • You can choose whether to make either optional in-app purchase.
  • You can contact terry.f.wang@gmail.com with privacy questions.

13. Security

Paper Gold minimizes the information it handles and uses Apple's system frameworks for local storage, private iCloud synchronization, and purchases. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely risk-free.

14. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Paper Gold's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected in the app or App Store information as appropriate.

摘要

Paper Gold 不要求建立 app 帳號、不顯示廣告、不跨 app 或網站追蹤使用者，也不使用一般用途的第三方分析工具。偏好設定與尚未完成的儀式進度會留在裝置上；當 Paper Gold 可以使用 iCloud 時，已完成的祭祀紀錄與成就解鎖會儲存在使用者的私人 iCloud 資料庫，並在其裝置間同步。Apple 處理選購的阿嬤金紙包與買命錢付款；RevenueCat 支援阿嬤金紙包，買命錢則由 Apple StoreKit 2 驗證。祭祀紀錄不會傳送給 RevenueCat，開發者也無法透過 CloudKit 查看使用者私人資料庫的內容。

範圍

本政策說明 Paper Gold 如何處理資訊。Paper Gold 是一款 iOS 文化互動與反思 app。本政策涵蓋 Paper Gold app 與本隱私權政策網站。Apple、iCloud、App Store、RevenueCat 與 GitHub 各自依其隱私權規範營運。

App 處理的資訊

Paper Gold 不要求你建立 app 帳號，也不要求提供姓名、email、電話、聯絡人、位置或廣告識別碼。App 只處理功能所需的資訊：本機偏好設定與尚未完成的進度、已完成的祭祀紀錄與成就，以及購買和權益狀態。

儲存在裝置本機的資料

Paper Gold 會在裝置上保存偏好設定與尚未完成的儀式狀態，包括音效、觸覺回饋、減少動畫、提高對比、語言、所選金紙、投入數量、所選祈願、介面使用的每日與累計數量，以及尚未完成的一次投入進度。這些本機資料不會傳送給開發者或 RevenueCat。刪除 app 會移除該裝置上的本機副本。依照 Apple 裝置設定，本機 app 資料也可能包含在 Apple 管理的裝置或 iCloud 備份中。

祭祀紀錄與成就的 iCloud 同步

當裝置已登入 iCloud，且 Paper Gold 的 iCloud 使用權限已開啟時，app 會使用 Apple CloudKit，將已完成的祭祀紀錄與成就解鎖儲存在使用者的私人 iCloud 資料庫。資料可以離線使用，並在登入相同 Apple Account 的裝置間同步。

一筆已完成的祭祀紀錄可能包含紀錄識別碼、日期與時間、燒化及疊數、金紙種類、祭祀對象、所選祈願、使用者輸入的自訂祈願文字，以及顯示祭祀帳簿所需的數值。成就紀錄包含成就類型與解鎖日期。

CloudKit 私人資料庫的內容可由使用者透過 app 存取，預設只會透過使用者的 Apple Account 存取。開發者無法在 CloudKit 開發者後台查看個別使用者私人資料庫的內容。Apple 作為 iCloud 服務提供者處理與儲存這些資料，資料會計入使用者的 iCloud 儲存空間。

如果 iCloud 無法使用，或使用者關閉 Paper Gold 的 iCloud 權限，app 仍可使用本機副本，但在 iCloud 再次可用前不會跨裝置同步。你可以在 Apple Account 的 iCloud 設定中控制哪些 app 使用 iCloud。

購買、Apple 與 RevenueCat

Paper Gold 提供兩筆分開的選購型一次性 App 內購買：「王豆腐阿嬤家的金紙包」與「買命錢」。兩筆付款均由 Apple 處理，Paper Gold 不會收到信用卡或銀行帳戶資料。

App 使用 RevenueCat SDK 驗證阿嬤金紙包、判斷其權益是否有效並支援恢復該筆購買；買命錢則直接使用 Apple StoreKit 2 載入商品、驗證交易並恢復使用權。RevenueCat 會取得 Apple 提供的購買紀錄與權益資訊，以及隨機產生的匿名 RevenueCat App User ID；也可能處理商品識別碼、商店、語系與幣別等有限的交易情境資訊。

這些購買資訊用於 app 功能、收據驗證、防止詐騙、顧客歷程與購買分析。Paper Gold 不會向 RevenueCat 提供祭祀紀錄、自訂祈願文字、聯絡資訊或廣告識別碼，也不會將購買資訊用於跨 app 廣告追蹤。

詳情請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策與 RevenueCat 隱私權政策。
