Paper Gold Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Paper Gold does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Data Collection

Paper Gold does not collect data from this app. The app does not require an account and does not send personal data to the developer or to third parties.

Local Device Storage

The app stores preferences and usage records locally on your device, such as sound settings, haptic settings, accessibility settings, burn counts, achievements, and ritual ledger records. This data stays on your device.

Tracking, Ads, and Analytics

Paper Gold does not use advertising tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Device Permissions

Paper Gold does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, health, or photo library permissions. Audio is generated and played locally, and the app does not use the microphone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect data, this privacy policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through tofus.substack.com.

Paper Gold 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 6 月 24 日

Paper Gold 不會收集、儲存或傳送個人資料。

App 會在你的裝置本機儲存偏好設定與使用紀錄，例如音效設定、觸覺回饋設定、輔助使用設定、燒化次數、成就與儀式紀錄。這些資料只會留在你的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者或第三方。

Paper Gold 不需要登入帳號，不使用廣告追蹤、第三方分析工具或第三方廣告 SDK。

Paper Gold 不要求相機、麥克風、定位、聯絡人、健康或照片圖庫權限。音效會在本機產生並播放，App 不使用麥克風。

如果未來版本加入會收集資料的功能，本隱私權政策與 App Store 隱私資訊會在功能上線前更新。

若有隱私相關問題，請透過 tofus.substack.com 聯絡開發者。
